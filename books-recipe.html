A Favourite Recipe: Vegetable Poha

Poha is a simple breakfast made with flattened rice and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flattened rice (poha)
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 small potato, chopped (optional)
  • 1 green chilli
  • Mustard seeds and curry leaves
  • Salt, turmeric and lemon juice
  • Oil and chopped coriander

Preparation Steps

  1. Rinse the poha briefly and let it soften.
  2. Heat oil in a pan. Add mustard seeds and curry leaves.
  3. Add onion, chilli and potato. Cook until soft.
  4. Add turmeric, salt and softened poha.
  5. Cook gently. Finish with lemon juice and coriander.
  6. Serve warm.
