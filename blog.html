My Journey into Web Development

Published on 6 November 2026 by Govind Nagar. In this article, I share my experiences and insights as I learn web development, including the challenges I faced and the resources that helped me along the way.

Starting with the Basics

HTML helps me organise information into headings, paragraphs, lists, images, links and tables. Learning these elements gives me a strong foundation.

Learning by Building

Small projects help me connect concepts with practical work. A personal website is useful because it includes navigation, multiple pages, images and tables.

What I Want to Learn Next

After HTML, I want to learn CSS for presentation and JavaScript for interactivity. My goal is to keep practising and build useful projects.
